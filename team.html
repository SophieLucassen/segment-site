The team

Segment is a two-person studio. You'll work with both of us.

Sophie Lucassen

Founder

Sophie founded Segment and does the work itself: the marketing, the design, the copy, the websites. Her background is in B2B and tech marketing, and she works close to the people she's building for, hands-on rather than advising from a distance.

Chris Blin

Co-founder

Chris is the strategic mind behind Segment. He's the one thinking about where a piece of work needs to go and who it should reach, and he makes the warm introductions that turn into the right conversations. Sophie runs the delivery; Chris makes sure it's pointed in the right direction.
