Social Media Management

Overview

We managed social media and advertising accounts for a range of Guernsey businesses including Little Big Brew Co, IslandFM, Peninsula Hotel, Canvas, Douvres Hotel, and Guernsey Together Festival.

The challenge

Each business had different audiences, different tones of voice, and different goals. A craft brewery's Instagram needs a completely different approach than a hotel's, and a radio station's content calendar looks nothing like a nightclub's. We needed to switch between brand voices daily.

Our approach

We developed distinct content strategies for each client, created brand-specific content calendars, and managed the day-to-day posting, community management, and paid advertising across Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. Photography, design, and copy were all handled in-house.
