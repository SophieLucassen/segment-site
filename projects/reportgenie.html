ReportGenie, Social Content

Overview

We designed and wrote the social media content for ReportGenie, a regulatory reporting tool that automates FATCA and CRS submissions. Content was published across LinkedIn and Mastodon.

The challenge

Making compliance software interesting on social media is hard. The audience (accounts managers, compliance officers) is niche, the subject matter is dry, and the competition for attention is fierce. We needed to make people stop scrolling for a regulatory reporting tool.

Our approach

We used a mix of pain-point led messaging ("Still stuck with manual reporting workflows?"), customer testimonials with real metrics, and clear step-by-step product demonstrations. The visual style mixed the ReportGenie brand colours with bold typography that stood out in feeds.
