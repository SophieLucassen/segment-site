TrueAxis AI, Launch Strategy & TrueLite

Overview

TrueAxis AI Labs had built a hospitality-focused AI product called TrueLite and needed a structured plan to take it to market. We developed the full go-to-market strategy covering positioning, target audience definition, messaging framework, and launch roadmap. We also designed and wrote the TrueLite landing page at trueaxisai.com/truelite.

The challenge

AI products in hospitality face a trust problem. The industry is people-first and often tech-resistant. TrueLite needed positioning that made it feel like a practical tool that makes people's jobs easier, not a replacement for human judgment. The landing page needed to communicate this instantly to Guernsey business owners who don't have dedicated HR, recruitment, or compliance support.

Our approach

We started with audience research to understand the actual decision-makers in hospitality and small business operations. From there, we built a positioning framework that led with outcomes rather than technology. "Get clear answers on the things that slow your business down" became the core message, tested against real business operators to make sure it landed without jargon.

For the landing page, we designed and wrote the full page with a clear value proposition, feature breakdown, and a frictionless "Try TrueLite for free" call to action. The page was built specifically for Guernsey businesses, referencing local context like Population Management requirements to build immediate relevance.
