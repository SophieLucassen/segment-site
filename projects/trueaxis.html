TrueAxis AI, Launch Strategy

Overview

TrueAxis AI Labs had built a hospitality-focused AI product and needed a structured plan to take it to market. We developed the full go-to-market strategy covering positioning, target audience definition, messaging framework, and launch roadmap.

The challenge

AI products in hospitality face a trust problem. The industry is people-first and often tech-resistant. The GTM strategy needed to position the product as a practical tool that makes people's jobs easier, not a replacement for human judgment.

Our approach

We started with audience research to understand the actual decision-makers in hospitality operations. From there, we built a positioning framework that led with outcomes rather than technology. The messaging was tested against real hospitality operators to make sure it landed without jargon.
