Cortex, Awareness Ads

Overview

Alongside the Bragi OOH campaign, we created general awareness advertising for Cortex Intelligent Systems, the company behind Bragi. These ads ran at local sportsgrounds to build brand recognition across the Guernsey business community.

The challenge

Cortex is a software and data company, but much of their local audience doesn't interact with tech products directly. The ads needed to be accessible enough for a general audience while still communicating credibility to the tech-literate viewers who might become clients.

Our approach

We kept the visuals bold and the messaging simple. The Cortex brand identity, with its circuit-board aesthetic, did most of the heavy lifting visually. The copy focused on what Cortex does at the broadest level rather than technical specifics, letting the brand design signal the sophistication.
