Bragi, B2B Social Content

Overview

We created the social media content strategy and ongoing design and copy for Bragi's LinkedIn presence. This included case studies, feature announcements, product launches, and point-of-view posts targeted at a technical B2B audience.

The challenge

B2B social content for data infrastructure products risks being either too dry (feature specs) or too vague (buzzword soup). Bragi needed content that demonstrated genuine technical understanding while remaining engaging enough to stop someone mid-scroll.

Our approach

We developed a content mix of case studies with real metrics, feature explainers using clear visual diagrams, and occasional lighter posts that showed personality. Each piece was designed to work as both a standalone visual and a conversation starter in the comments.
