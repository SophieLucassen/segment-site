Trollverse, Engagement Dashboard

Overview

We designed and validated a user engagement dashboard for Trollverse, an NFT gaming company that gamifies real-world sustainable behaviours. The project involved full UX research and an iterative design process, delivering data-backed recommendations that were adopted by the client.

The challenge

Trollverse needed a dashboard that made complex engagement data understandable to players while encouraging continued participation in sustainability challenges. The data included carbon credits, tribe performance, personal progress, and social features, all of which needed to coexist without overwhelming users.

Our approach

We led with user research to understand what players actually cared about, then prioritised the metrics that drove engagement. The dashboard was designed with a clear hierarchy: personal stats first, tribe performance second, social features third. Each iteration was tested and refined based on user feedback.
