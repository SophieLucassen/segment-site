Girls Who Innovate, STEM Initiative

Overview

We managed the end-to-end planning and delivery of the Girls Who Innovate event, a partnership between Cortex Technologies and Girlguiding Guernsey for the International Day of Women and Girls in STEM.

The challenge

Cortex wanted to give back to the community in a way that was genuine and impactful, not just a branding exercise. The event needed to genuinely inspire girls aged 10-14 about technology while also generating positive press coverage for Cortex.

Our approach

We planned the entire event from initial concept to delivery: budgeting, activity design, venue logistics, partnerships, promotional materials, and media outreach. Activities included Bloxels game design, hands-on engineering challenges, and presentations from women in the tech industry. We designed the event programme and all print materials.
