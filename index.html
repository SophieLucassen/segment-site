About Segment

We've spent the last five years marketing B2B tech and SaaS companies, alongside businesses across hospitality, recruitment, media, and more. Our work spans website copy, campaigns, social media, events, and translating complex products into clear, jargon-free messaging.

Not every business needs marketing full-time. A permanent hire is a significant commitment, and many companies either aren't ready for one or don't need one all year round.

Fractional marketing is the alternative: an experienced marketing function for the hours you need it, without the salary, recruitment, or management a permanent role involves. It scales with the business, expanding around a launch and easing back when things are quieter, so you only pay for the work itself.
